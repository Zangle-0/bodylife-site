FAQ

Is Body Life a medical app?

No. Body Life is a personal record and habit companion. It does not diagnose, treat or recommend medication. Scales are presented as personal trends with a non-diagnosis note, and the full text of every practice lists its source.

Where do the practices come from?

Each practice is registered against an official or professional source — public-health agencies, medical institutions or peer-reviewed papers — checked against the original text before it ships. Anything we write ourselves is built on one of those anchors.

Do I need an account?

No. Recording works fully offline with no account. An account is only needed if you subscribe and turn on sync.

Which languages are supported?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean. More may follow.

When does it launch?

Android first, on Google Play, once store verification completes. An iOS version is not planned at this time.
